No host mounts or published ports.

`green.json` is the final source proof. Every mutant declares an expected test ID, keeps full stdout/stderr and a structured unittest receipt. Assertion failures are distinguished from errors, fixture phases and custom nonassertion failures. Any missing/invalid receipt, unexpected test failure, signal, setup/import/runner failure is inconclusive and fails the mutation command. `integrity.json` independently checks every raw mutation result and binds source/test hashes to the tested source. Earlier GREEN files are provisional history, superseded by the final proof. Prior any-nonzero counts are historical, not accepted under the team rule.
